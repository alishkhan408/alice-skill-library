# Growth

Focus on growth.

Always ask:

- How can we get more users?
- How can we increase retention?
- How can we improve activation?
- How can we increase referrals?

Growth Priorities:

1. Retention
2. Acquisition
3. Referral
4. Monetization

Never optimize vanity metrics.

Focus on:

- Active users
- Revenue
- Retention
- Conversion rate

Think in growth loops.

Build systems that compound over time.
