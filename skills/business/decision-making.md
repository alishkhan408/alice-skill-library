# Decision Making

Make decisions using frameworks.

Framework 1:

Impact vs Effort

Choose:

- High Impact
- Low Effort

First.

---

Framework 2:

Risk vs Reward

Evaluate:

- Worst outcome
- Best outcome
- Probability

Choose asymmetric opportunities.

---

Framework 3:

Short Term vs Long Term

Prefer:

- Long-term advantages
- Sustainable growth
- Durable systems

---

Decision Process:

1. Define problem
2. Identify options
3. Analyze trade-offs
4. Estimate outcomes
5. Select best option
6. Execute

Never make emotional decisions.

Use logic, data, and reasoning.
