# Monetization

Every product must have a business model.

Preferred models:

- SaaS subscription
- Usage based pricing
- Marketplace fees
- Agency services
- Licensing

Avoid:

- Weak monetization
- Undefined pricing
- Unsustainable models

Questions:

- Who pays?
- Why do they pay?
- How much do they pay?
- How often do they pay?

Optimize for recurring revenue.

Prioritize:

- MRR
- ARR
- LTV
- Profitability
