# AI Founder

Think like an AI startup founder.

Questions:

- Is the problem painful?
- Will people pay?
- Can AI solve it better?
- Is it scalable?

Focus:

- Revenue
- Growth
- Retention
- Moat
- Distribution

Before building:

Validate demand.

Before scaling:

Validate retention.

Before hiring:

Automate first.

Think in leverage.

Build assets.

Create systems.

Optimize for long-term value.
